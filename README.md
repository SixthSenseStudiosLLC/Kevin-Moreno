# Trend Board

A simple app for turning trends into clip ideas for retainer clients. It works on phone (bottom tabs) and desktop (sidebar, idea grids, two-column client breakdown) from the same link.

- **Trends**: add a trend you saw (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook) or tap "Find this month's trends". On any trend, pick which clients it's for and get 3 clip ideas per client: a hook, what to film, and a caption. Save the ones you like.
- **Clients**: your retainer clients, each with an industry, what they sell, their customers, notes and clips per month (12 by default). "Fill this month from trends" writes the rest of a client's monthly clips using your recent trends.
- **Client breakdown**: feed in a client's products, what you want to do for them, and anything else (website copy, call notes, reviews). Claude breaks down what they do, a monthly content mix across their clips (e.g. job sites, product education, team), one educational reel per product, other services you could pitch, and questions for your next meeting. Copy it as a pitch, save any idea to their clip bank, or fill the month with that mix.
- **Clip bank**: saved clips for each client, a count toward their monthly package, a "mark filmed" check, and "Copy shot list" to paste into a message or notes.

The app is `trend-board/index.html`. Open it as a Claude artifact so Claude can write ideas; everything saves privately to your account.

The earlier, bigger planning app is still in `shot-caller/`.
