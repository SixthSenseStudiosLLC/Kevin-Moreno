# Shot Caller

A phone-first pre-production app for content creators.

- **Ideas**: set your niche, audience, platform and business goal once, pick a lens (trend format, industry news, behind the scenes, myth vs fact…), and pull 6 fresh video ideas at a time.
- **Plan**: turn any idea into a shoot plan with a 3-second hook, timecoded beat sheet, numbered shot list (shot size, A/B-roll, notes), gear list, location and permission checks, and a ready-to-post caption.
- **Field**: a big-tap checklist for the shoot. It highlights the next shot and has a gear check and a keep-screen-on toggle.
- **Library**: your saved plans and idea bank.

The app lives in `shot-caller/index.html`. Opened as a Claude artifact, it uses Claude to write ideas and plans and saves them privately to your account. Opened as a plain file in a browser, it falls back to a built-in idea deck and saves to that browser.
