# Shot Caller

A phone-first planning app for a content creator who runs a retainer business with local business owners: short-form content plus a business-owner podcast.

- **Month**: calendar of shoot and post dates, retainer clients with monthly deliverables (short-form and podcast episodes), large projects, booked revenue against a monthly goal, and "Plan this month" for each retainer client (Claude batches ideas into shoot weeks).
- **Ideas**: switch between your industries, target one focus area, pick a lens (trend format, industry news, behind the scenes, myth vs fact, crossover of two industries…) and pull 6 ideas at a time. Ideas take your logged results and recent trends into account.
- **Trends**: log trends you spot on TikTok, Instagram or Facebook. Claude adapts each one to your industries, and trends under 3 weeks old feed into new ideas.
- **Niches**: type any industry and map it into 12 sub-niches, including unexpected ones. Go deeper on any branch, add focus areas, or save a sub-niche as its own industry. Health, hormone and finance niches are flagged and plans carry claims warnings.
- **Shoot**: short-form plans (hook, beat sheet, shot list, gear, caption) and podcast episode plans (run of show, questions for the guest, setup, guest prep, clips to cut). Set shoot and post dates, client and status. Field mode is a big-tap checklist for shots or interview questions.
- **Results & Patterns**: log views, watch %, engagement, follows and leads per video. Patterns break results down by niche, hook style, format, posting day and platform, and Claude can read your patterns and suggest what to try next.

The app lives in `shot-caller/index.html`. Opened as a Claude artifact, it uses Claude to write maps, ideas and plans and saves everything privately to your account. Opened as a plain file in a browser, it uses built-in templates and saves to that browser.
