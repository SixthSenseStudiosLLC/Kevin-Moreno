Film your own content, right now, today.

Maybe you're not ready to bring in a content team. Maybe you're not sure what it would look like to have someone help you plan, shoot or edit. That's okay. You can start on your own, today, for free, with the phone already in your pocket.

Everything we know about getting started is on this page, in simple steps. Nothing to buy and no experience needed. Take what helps you, skip what doesn't, and just begin.

Get your phone ready

Just aim and press record.

That's really it. If you want, three small things help:

  • Wipe the lens with your shirt.
  • Hold your phone tall, not sideways.
  • Don’t zoom. Walk closer instead.

Pick your spot

  • Stand facing a window. The light makes you look your best.
  • Don’t stand with a window behind you. You will look like a dark shadow.
  • Pick a quiet room. Turn off fans and music.
  • Prop your phone up so the camera is at the same height as your eyes.
  • Stand about one big step away, so people can see your face and shoulders.

What to say

Short videos work best when they follow this order. Think about it before you press record.

  • Start strongSay something interesting right away, like the best part or a question.
  • Show itLet people see what you do: your work, your space, your product.
  • Say whyTell them in one sentence how it helps them.
  • What to do nextTell them how to reach you, or to follow for more.

Film a few extra shots

These are little clips you can drop into your video so it isn't just you talking the whole time.

  • Film your hands at work, your tools and your products.
  • Film your sign, your door and your street.
  • Hold each shot still for five seconds.
  • Get ten of these every time. They make your videos more fun to watch.

Ideas to film

Not sure what to make? Tap the button and we'll pick one for you.

Today, film: Tap the button to get an idea

Or pick any of these. They work for almost any business.

  • Who you are and why you started
  • A tour of your space
  • How you make it, start to finish
  • A before and after
  • A question customers always ask
  • A day in your life
  • A quick tip only you would know
  • Meet your team
  • A happy customer (ask them first)
  • Your favorite spot in town

Put it together

  • Use a free app like CapCut or InShot, or the editor already on your phone.
  • Cut out the quiet parts at the start and the end.
  • Add words on the screen. Lots of people watch with the sound off.
  • Keep it short: under one minute is great.

You're ready. Press record!
