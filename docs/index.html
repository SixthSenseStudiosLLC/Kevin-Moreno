Sixth Sense Studios: North Jersey's video partner for content, events and podcasts

Welcome to Sixth Sense Studios, home of Reliable Kev and your local video partner in New Jersey. I specialize in monthly content, business owner podcast, events coverage and brand films & projects for towns, downtowns, local businesses and trade companies throughout Bergen County and Passaic County and Essex County and Morris County.

Trust is the most valuable thing a business has. People want to see who they are hiring before they call. I tell the full story: the work, the people behind it, and the town it belongs to, so your customers feel like they already know you.

Why choose Sixth Sense Studios for your video?

Every kind of video, one person

Monthly content, podcasts, events and brand films. You work with me from the first call to the final cut, so nothing gets lost in a hand-off.

Local trust and full context

I film the business, the owner and the town around them. Neighbors choose local, and they want to see who they are hiring before they call.

Built around your week

Shoot days are planned and batched, so filming never takes over your business. You get a steady stream of content without the hassle.

  • Based in North Jersey, filming in your town
  • One person from first call to final cut
  • Free planning call before anything is filmed
