About Reliable Kev Media

Reliable Kev Media is a video production company that has served North Jersey since 2022. We produce monthly content, podcasts and Q&As, event coverage and brand films for municipalities, downtowns, local businesses and trade companies.

Meet Kev

I have never been someone who's comfortable in front of the camera. Even recording myself, I've carried a sense of insecurity and self-doubt that I have always struggled with.

Through my own effort, I've learned what it means to put yourself in a place where you have to trust yourself, and trust someone else to capture you.

Trust yourself, and trust someone to capture you.

That's where this is going, and it's why I tell other people's stories. I've never been someone who connects easily with groups. I connect with people one on one. That's why so much of my work is filming one-on-one with business owners, letting them tell their story.

A lot of people just want help with their business. To me, that's not the right place to start. The approach I bring is the full context: how you started, and why you're doing this in the first place. Documentary style.

The foundation

Reliable Kev Media was not built in a film school, an agency or a marketing firm. We do not sell advertising campaigns or chase algorithms. We offer the ability to sit with a client, understand what matters to them and tell that story with clarity.

That is the foundation of Reliable Kev Media. Every project begins with listening. The direction, the shots and the edit all follow from your story, not from a template.

With every business we film, we look for the complete arc: where you began, the work it required and where you are headed.

Behind the camera

Full context storytelling

We invest the time, care and thought required to understand your story, and then direct and shape it on camera. Trust is a business's most valuable asset, and prospective clients want to know whom they are hiring before they call. For that reason, we never film the product alone. Every project tells three stories:

  • The business. What you produce, how you work and why customers return.
  • The owner. Your story, in your own words, so customers know whom they are buying from.
  • The town. The street, the neighbors and the community you belong to.

Who we work with

  • Municipalities informing residents about what is being built, repaired and planned
  • Downtown associations and chambers of commerce that want to bring visitors to their streets
  • Local retailers, restaurants and service providers
  • Trades and contractors: concrete, construction, landscaping and more

The gear

  • Two Canon 90D cameras, so interviews and podcasts are covered from two angles
  • A range of lenses, tripods and lighting
  • DJI Flip drone for aerial footage

Our standards for quality

  • A plan for every shoot, so no time is wasted
  • Respect for your time, your staff and your customers on the day of filming
  • Polished edits delivered on schedule, ready to publish
