Monthly Content

Short-form clips every month, batched into shoot days so it never eats your week.

What's included:

  • A plan for the month before anything is filmed
  • Batched shoot days, minimal disruption to your business
  • Edited clips delivered ready to post

Business Owner Podcast

A podcast episode filmed on location at your business, then cut into clips.

What's included:

  • Filmed where you work, not in a studio
  • Full episode plus short clips from every recording

Events Coverage

Openings, festivals, town events and ribbon cuttings, captured from start to finish.

What's included:

  • Grand openings, festivals, fundraisers and town events

  • Photos and clips for social, press and next year

Brand Films & Projects

Brand films, launches and one-off pieces that show the whole picture.

What's included:

  • Brand films and about-us videos
  • Launches, job-site and before-and-after pieces
  • Public project updates for towns and municipalities

Set rate or custom quote?

Set rate

You want me to come film: an event, a property, your business and a few questions with you. Little planning, clear deliverables, fixed price.

Custom quote

Frequently asked questions

How long have you been doing this?

Since 2022. I've spent the last few years filming towns, local businesses and trade companies across North Jersey.

How long until I get my video?

Most projects are delivered in 2–3 weeks, depending on the scope of work. Bigger projects get a timeline agreed before we start.

How much does it cost?

A half day (up to 4 hours) is $500 and includes your choice of one long-form YouTube video or three short clips. A full day (up to 8 hours) is $1,100 and includes a planning call, a sit-down Q&A with you, one long-form video and three short clips. Adding drone footage is $300 more. Brand films, the podcast and monthly content get a custom quote.

What's the difference between a set rate and a custom quote?

A set rate is for when you want me to come film: an event, a property, or your business and a few questions with you. A custom quote is for full storytelling projects like brand films, the podcast and monthly content, which take more planning, directing and editing.

Do I get the raw footage?

Yes, on request. Your rate includes the edited videos listed, and the raw footage is yours if you ask for it.

Can I get my video faster?

Every project gets the same care, so edited videos are delivered in 2–3 weeks. Rush delivery is not offered.

Can I add more videos or hours?

Yes. Anything beyond what is included, like extra videos, extra hours or extra revisions, is quoted before any work begins, so you always know the price up front.

What gear do you use?

Two Canon 90D cameras with a range of lenses, tripods and lighting, plus a DJI Flip drone for aerial shots. Two cameras means interviews and podcasts are covered from two angles.

Where do you film?

All over North Jersey: Bergen, Passaic, Essex and Morris County. If you are a little outside that, ask.

Do I need to be comfortable on camera?

No. I direct the shoot and keep it relaxed, so you can just talk about what you know. No script and no camera experience needed.

What happens after I reach out?

We start with a free planning call. I learn about your business and what you want people to know, then send you a plan and a price.

Can I combine services?

Yes, and most clients do. A podcast episode can feed a month of clips, and event coverage can turn into a brand film. Drone footage can be added to any of them.
