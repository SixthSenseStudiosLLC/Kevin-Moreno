How an event day goes

A detailed schedule is not required; many clients do not have one in advance. Please share your start and end times and any moments that must be captured, and we will cover the day in the following order:

  1. SetupThe venue, the setup and the details before guests arrive.
  2. ArrivalsGuests arriving, greetings and the atmosphere building.
  3. Key momentsSpeeches, ribbon cuttings, performances and any moments you identify, as they happen.
  4. ClosingThe final moments and the venue as the event concludes.

Frequently asked questions

Is my first video really free?

Yes. One short vertical video (up to 60 seconds), open to anyone new to Reliable Kev Media. It is simple by design, with nothing to plan or prepare: we arrive, film on location in North Jersey and deliver the edit with music and captions, ready to publish. One per client. There is no contract and no obligation. It is the simplest way to experience how we work.

How long have you been in business?

Since 2022. Reliable Kev Media has spent the past several years filming municipalities, local businesses and trade companies across North Jersey.

When will I receive my video?

Most projects are delivered within 2–3 weeks, depending on the scope of work. Larger projects receive an agreed timeline before production begins.

How much does it cost?

A single video is $150: one short vertical video, filmed, edited and ready to publish. For events, a half day (up to 4 hours) starts at $500 and includes five short vertical clips. A full day (up to 8 hours) starts at $1,100 and includes a 2–3 minute highlight video and six short vertical clips. Drone footage can be added from $300. All other projects, including brand films, sit-down interviews with owners, podcasts and monthly content, are priced by custom quote. You will always receive a clear price before any filming begins.

What is the difference between a package and a custom quote?

A package (a single video, or half-day or full-day event coverage) is designed for clients who need filming with defined deliverables and a set price. A custom quote covers all other work, such as brand films, sit-down interviews with owners, podcasts and monthly content, which require additional planning, direction and editing.

Do I receive the raw footage?

Your price includes the edited videos listed. Raw footage is not included, but it can be added to any project on request and will be reflected in your quote.

Can my video be delivered sooner?

Every project receives the same level of care, so edited videos are delivered within 2–3 weeks. Rush delivery is not available.

Can I add more videos or hours?

Yes. Any work beyond the included scope, such as additional videos, hours or revisions, is quoted before it begins, so you always know the price in advance.

What equipment do you use?

Two Canon 90D cameras with a range of lenses, tripods and lighting, as well as a DJI Flip drone for aerial footage. Two cameras allow interviews and podcasts to be filmed from two angles.

Where do you film?

Throughout North Jersey, including Bergen, Passaic, Essex, Morris and Hudson counties. If your location is slightly outside this area, please contact us.

Do I need to be comfortable on camera?

No. We direct every shoot and keep the process relaxed, so you can simply speak about what you know. No script or on-camera experience is required.

What happens after I contact you?

We begin with a complimentary consultation to learn about your business and what you would like people to know. We then provide a plan and a price.

Can I combine services?

Yes, and many clients do. A podcast episode can supply a month of clips, and event coverage can become a brand film. Drone footage can be added to any service.
